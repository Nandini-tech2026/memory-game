About Memory Match

Memory Match is a free card game that tests your memory. Flip two cards at a time and find all 8 pairs in 15 moves or fewer.

How to play

Enter a nickname and tap Start. The cards show for 2 seconds, then flip back. Match all pairs before you run out of moves. Your best scores appear on the leaderboard.
